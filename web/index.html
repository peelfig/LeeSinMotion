Why LeeSin Motion?

Extract: Segment

Analyze easing and value changes between two keyframes for precise handoff.

Extract: Single Key

Capture exact absolute time points of specific keyframes in your timeline.

Scan: Current Layer

Bulk export every animation property and keyframe pair for an entire layer.

Scan: Pre-comp

Generate a full animation script ordered by element appearance time.

Seamless Workflow

  • 01

    Select Keyframes

    Highlight the start and end keyframes of any animation in your timeline.

  • 02

    Click to Capture

    Hit the "Get Specs" button. LeeSinMotion parses the data instantly.

  • 03

    Share with Eng

    Paste the generated specs directly into Jira, Notion, or your handoff documentation.
